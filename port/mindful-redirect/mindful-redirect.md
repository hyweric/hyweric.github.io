### Synopsis:

A Chrome extension website blocker that focuses on encouraging mindful usage rather than completely preventing access. It redirects specified websites onto a page, where you can choose to exit, redirect, or access the site through the means of completing a task.

Although this extension is available on the Chrome Extension Store, it was built as a personal tool, tailored to my own needs. I haven't spent much time promoting or polishing it, which is why the styling may appear a bit unrefined, almost a dreamlike nature. However, beneath the rough exterior, there are many valuable topics like JS and CSS animations I've learned throughout the development process.

### Why I built it

During a school break, I found myself wasting countless hours idly browsing the web. Typical website blockers simply didn't work for me - I would end up disabling them whenever I needed to access a certain website, and then forget to turn them back on. Previously, I had created a simple [Chrome extension that redirected YouTube links to a distraction-free](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/youtube-redirect-distract/ldlallggfghaaoaghhecgeebpddcpdhl), no-cookie alternative, which significantly reduced my time spent on YouTube. Seeing the approach worked, I decided to create a website blocker that focuses on encouraging mindful usage rather than completely preventing access.
