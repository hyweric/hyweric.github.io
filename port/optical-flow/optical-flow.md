## Overview

While working on a autonomous drone, I needed a localization algorithm designed to be cost-effective and easily implementable with just a single camera mounted on the bottom of the drone. The algorithm utilizes the Lucas Kanade optical flow algorithm to track an the drone’s position relative to the environment (Egomotion). The investigation focuses on assessing the feasibility of this approach across various environmental conditions. It ultimately wasn’t able to work because of the slow processing times live but I thought I’d include it since I compiled quite a bit data during testing.
