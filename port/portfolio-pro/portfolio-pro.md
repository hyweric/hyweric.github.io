### Synopsis:

AI website Maker using your Resume

Personal Websites are becoming more widespread, as a critical part of personal branding, but many people don’t have the knowledge of how to create a website or the time to make it. This website makes you a personal website using AI, specifically the Google Gemini API. Users can upload their resume and profile picture to have their website will automatically generated, and save their info as well. 

- Project built as a AP CS A final project and Hackathon Submission.
