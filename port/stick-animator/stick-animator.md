### Synopsis:

A website that transforms an input video into an animated stick character video, built upon on a personal tool I wrote a few years ago.  It can be used by creators to make anonymous faceless YouTube videos, by teachers to create entertaining video lessons, or anywhere else where a stick figure video is applicable.
